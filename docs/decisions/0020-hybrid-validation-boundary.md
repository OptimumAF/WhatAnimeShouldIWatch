**Status:** Protocol fixed on 2026-09-27 before inspecting hybrid weight metrics. M5.4 may be checked only after implementation, isolation tests, and the full synthetic/mock gate pass; this fixture cannot establish production ranking quality.

## Fixed model, people, and choice

Use decision 0018's validated fit-only raw snapshot, train-only `graph-two-epochs` item model and positive pair graph. Its four disjoint invented new-user validation profiles supply ten score-independent, nested 1/3/5/10 observation prefixes and two separate validation ratings each. The item model, graph, catalog, metadata, supplied order, relevance threshold of raw score ≥7, top K of 10, and franchise option off are fixed before blend selection. Do not tune model parameters again from these new-user labels.

The candidate specification lists model weights `0`, `0.25`, `0.5`, `0.75`, and `1`. Each weight uses the browser's `buildGraphRecommendationsForPreferences`, `buildModelRecommendationsForPreferences`, `createCandidateEligibilityPolicy`, and `rankEligibleCandidates("hybrid", ...)`. If the model has no eligible mapped output, use the browser's graph, then labeled catalog-coverage fallback. Apply the final franchise selector after eligibility. Score the **displayed** eligible order; a missing component's effective weight and endpoint candidate sets are whatever the browser ranker returns. Do not credit graph or coverage fallback to model-only performance. Retain the separate decision 0018 model-only and warm-user reports.

Choose the weight with the highest mean NDCG@10 over all measurable user-prefix cases, giving each case equal weight. A case is measurable only when at least one raw-positive validation title passes the central eligibility policy; disclose omitted cases and eligible-positive counts. For an exact metric tie, choose the smaller model weight. Also record Hit@10, Recall@10, per-prefix NDCG@10, and engine/fallback counts as diagnostics; they do not select the weight. Do not use the prior authored min-max comparison or any test label as a selection objective. This weight is an experiment record, not a new product default.

## Frozen report boundary

Check in a distinct invented four-user final cohort with the same 24-title metadata snapshot and no overlap with fit or validation people. Its user scores are not parsed or scored by selection. The selection command may hash its raw bytes solely to bind the future report input, analogous to decision 0017's snapshot binding. Freeze the original absolute selection/report paths, exact candidate specification, fit/model/metadata fingerprints, validation and final-fixture byte hashes, ordered validation trials, chosen weight, and an integrity digest. Keep the selection and report outside public and release assets.

The separate final-report command verifies the frozen record, model and validation input, then creates a one-use marker exclusively **before parsing or scoring** the reserved final cohort. It checks the final byte hash and disjoint identities after the marker; any failure consumes that report attempt for investigation. A copied/tampered selection, changed model/fixture, reused marker/report, or public output must fail. Do not run this command on the reserved final cohort during M5.4: M5.6–M5.7 still need predeclared baseline and reporting discipline. Focused tests may use a different temporary invented final cohort to prove the one-use flow.

## Acceptance and limits

Test exact browser ranker use at the 0/1 endpoints, effective sole-component behavior, watched/imported-seen/exclude/Include Only and metadata filters, and final selector order. Editing validation labels must leave model scores and candidate lists unchanged while changing label-derived metrics. Editing only the reserved final fixture with a refreshed hash must leave validation trials and the chosen weight unchanged. Reject malformed candidate spaces, stale manifest/model/metadata fingerprints, overlapping evaluation users, and output-path reuse. Verify a marker exists before any temporary final test parsing/scoring and duplicate reports fail. Run the full fixture, typecheck, unit, Python, workflow, build, browser, and fresh PR CI checks. No source-use hold is lifted and no selected synthetic weight is promoted to runtime.
