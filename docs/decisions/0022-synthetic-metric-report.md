## Predeclared measures

- Compute binary Recall@10/20 as positive labels in the displayed first K positions divided by eligible positive labels. Compute binary NDCG@10/20 from gains `1/log2(rank+1)` and the ideal gain for `min(K, eligible positive labels)`. Positions beyond a shorter displayed list are misses. Report both cutoffs for every method and supplied-prefix slice.
- Coverage@K is the count of distinct titles appearing in displayed top-K lists across **measurable** cases divided by the union of those cases' centrally eligible candidate IDs. Label this restricted validation catalog coverage, not global catalog or production coverage.
- Diversity@K is the equal-case mean of mean pairwise Jaccard genre distance among displayed top-K titles, using only the fixed authored metadata. A case with fewer than two displayed titles is unmeasurable for diversity and counted separately. This is genre spread, not franchise diversity or relevance.
- Popularity bias@K is the equal-case mean of (mean train-only raw rating count among displayed top-K titles minus mean train-only count in that case's eligible universe). Positive means concentration toward titles with more ratings in this restricted fit snapshot. Report raw count difference, not a global popularity or quality claim.
- The low-support evidence slice assigns each eligible positive candidate the maximum co-rater support of a **positive v1 pair-preference edge** connecting it to an explicitly Liked title in that case's supplied prefix. Missing Liked sources or positive edges have support 0. Low is support 0 or 1; higher is support 2 or more. Report eligible label, user, and case counts for both slices; compute the same Recall/NDCG cutoffs using only each slice's labels while keeping the full ranked list. Missing evidence is disclosed in the zero-support bucket, not treated as a negative preference.
- For sparse history, show the same measures by the existing 1/3/5/10 supplied prefixes. Prefixes are nested and correlated; changes across them are descriptive, not a causal effect of adding a rating.
- For uncertainty of primary mean displayed NDCG@10, use a 2,048-replicate percentile bootstrap with seed 271828. Resample **users**, retaining all their prefix cases together; never resample correlated prefixes independently. Report the 2.5th/97.5th percentiles with linear interpolation. Four invented users cannot establish a production confidence interval.
