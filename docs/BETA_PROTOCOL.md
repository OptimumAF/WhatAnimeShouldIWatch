# M10 beta protocol — predeclared preparation

Protocol version **1**, prepared 2026-10-05 for M10.1–M10.2. **No human beta session has run.** The invented demo and mocked browser journeys can rehearse the steps, but they cannot establish whether people find recommendations useful. Start human sessions only after the M0–M8 web release gates, provider source/use decisions, and a compatible candidate release have been reviewed. Record the exact build, data tag/bundle, catalog coverage, and protocol version before recruitment. A participant's consent to test does not grant provider collection, redistribution, or model-training rights.

## Session setup and consent

- Recruit eight new testers: one desktop and one physical mobile-device participant in each case below. Use self-reported experience to assign a case; do not request a username or copy a viewing list to classify someone. Keep each participant in one primary case. If recruitment or a supported device is unavailable, report the missing case instead of treating the smaller sample as the release gate.
- Use a fresh local test profile or a participant-approved existing browser profile. Explain before any reset that the app stores preferences and a watchlist locally. Never clear someone else's state without their permission and a recovery plan. Use invented fixture files for dry runs. A live beta may use a participant-controlled local list only after the source/use and release gates permit that flow; the facilitator never receives the file. Do not exercise username imports or a proxy as a substitute.
- No external telemetry, screen/audio recording, raw title list, rating, username, local file, or identifiable screenshot is collected by default. A separate explicit opt-in is needed for any recording or screenshot, with a reviewed storage/deletion plan. Keep notes in a restricted local location; publish only aggregate counts and redacted defects. If the participant declines, stop before taking notes.
- Read before opening the app: “We are testing whether this anime discovery app is understandable and useful. You may skip a question or stop at any time. Your saved list stays in this browser; the approved app may request public anime metadata. I may see titles on your screen, but I would record only task outcomes, counts, and comments you choose to share. I will not copy your anime list or record your screen. May I take those minimal notes for this session?” For a consenting session, record only **yes and date** before other notes. If consent is declined, stop without creating a session record. Ask separately whether short anonymous quotations may be retained; default to paraphrase. Stop note-taking if consent is withdrawn.
- Freeze the build and dataset for the round. Confirm the app labels the data source and actual recommendation engine. If the candidate cannot display at least ten eligible unseen titles for a case, still record the journey and coverage failure, but do not count that case toward the 3-of-10 usefulness target. Do not replace missing data with a silent sample.

## Four cases

| Case | Recruitment signal and starting path | What the case probes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sparse history | Knows one to three liked titles and at least one merely seen title; chooses manual favorites. | Whether a small, accurately marked preference set produces intelligible suggestions without treating Seen as Liked. |
| Broad history | Has a participant-held local list of at least 20 watched titles across several genres; chooses local import, reviews the preview, then applies it. Use an invented list in rehearsal. | Import preview, mapped/unmapped coverage, watched exclusions, and varied suggestions. |
| Niche taste | Can name three to ten liked titles concentrated in a narrow genre and one seen or disliked title; chooses manual selection. | Whether focused taste survives filters and the disliked/seen distinction without false confidence. |
| New to anime | Has no useful watch history; chooses Browse or seasonal ideas without adding a preference. | Whether exploration labels its nonpersonal signal and permits saving a choice without fabricating history. |

Do not supply titles or preferred answers during a human session. The eight-title invented demo is for path rehearsal only; its authored titles and rankings are not evidence of real-world plausibility.

## Neutral facilitator script

Give one prompt at a time. Allow up to three minutes per prompt, then mark it incomplete; a single verbatim repeat is allowed. Do not point at a control, explain a score, suggest a title, or complete an action for the tester until after the outcome is recorded. Record any hint as assisted and ask a non-leading “What did you expect?” after the task.

1. **Start (case-specific):** “Set this up using the starting information described for your case. Tell me when it reflects your choices.” For broad history add: “Please check what the local import says it will change before applying it.” For new to anime: “Start without entering a watch history.”
2. **Inspect:** “Find suggestions or ideas you might consider. Tell me why the first one appears and what you think its displayed score means.” Do not teach graph, model, catalog fallback, community score, or uncertainty during the task. Before the next prompt, ask: “Without naming them to me, how many of the first ten eligible unseen choices would you plausibly try?” Record a 0–10 count and the number shown, without titles.
3. **Filter:** “Narrow the choices using a genre, year, or score control. Tell me what changed and whether the app has enough information to apply that filter.” The participant chooses the filter; a truthful partial-coverage or no-match state is a valid observation.
4. **Correct:** “If you spot something already watched or unwanted, make sure it does not keep appearing as a suggestion.” If no such title appears, ask the tester to explain what they would do; do not create a negative signal on their behalf. Record Seen, Not interested, or other action separately.
5. **Save and return:** “Save one unseen possibility for later. Leave this view, reload or reopen the page, and find the saved title again.” Record whether the shortlist persisted and whether the item stayed out of current suggestions. Do not ask for an export of the watchlist.

After the tasks, ask which reasons or prerequisites were confusing. Record coded feedback, not title names. Ask whether any title already marked Seen or excluded in the app appeared in the first ten before filtering/saving; verify such a report locally with the participant, without copying their history. An unfamiliar title later recognized as watched but never marked in the app is a separate coverage report.

## Predeclared success and stop rules (M10.2)

Freeze these rules before the first human session. The round is a small diagnostic beta, not a population-quality estimate or a model comparison.

- **Core journey:** An unassisted pass requires the case's starting path, suggestion inspection, one filter, a saved unseen title, and retrieval after reload/reopen, with no facilitator hint or silent data loss. For broad history, preview-before-apply is required. At least **6 of 8** sessions must pass, with **at least one pass in every case** and at least one on each device class.
- **Explanation:** A pass identifies the actual source of the displayed result (personal evidence or a labeled exploration/fallback signal) and understands that rank points or community scores are not calibrated personal probabilities. At least **6 of 8** sessions must pass, with at least one in every case. Record the app's requested and actual engines so fallback is not scored as a model explanation.
- **Plausible unseen choices:** Every session needs ten eligible unseen titles visible before filtering/saving to assess this release target. The starting target is **at least 3 plausible choices among the first 10** by that participant's own judgment. At least **6 of 8** assessable sessions must meet it, with at least one in every case. Fewer than ten eligible titles, unknown coverage, or an unapproved dataset is **not assessable**, never counted as success; report the denominator and keep the beta gate open.
- **Eligibility and trust:** **Zero** titles already marked Seen/watched, Plan to Watch, or Not interested in local state may appear in the displayed top ten. A crash, lost local state, unauthorized provider/proxy request, or unintended private-data exposure is a stop-and-fix defect, not averaged into an overall score. A participant-recalled but unrecorded watch is diagnostic feedback, not proof of an eligibility leak.
- **Decision:** If any threshold or safety condition fails, log the case and reproduction using invented equivalents, link the defect to a plan task/regression, fix it, and run a new declared round. Do not silently change the thresholds, mix pre/post-fix sessions, or announce a release from an incomplete round. Even a passing small round needs the technical gates and owner review before release.

## Minimal restricted session record

Use a random session code with no identity mapping in the repository. For consenting sessions, record: protocol/build/bundle IDs; case and device class; consent yes/date; whether any separate quotation opt-in was given; starting path and actual engine; each prompt's unassisted/assisted/incomplete outcome and elapsed minute; explanation pass with one brief paraphrase; eligible first-list count and plausible count; app-marked exclusion leaks; local persistence result; feedback codes (already-seen, disliked, confusing, irrelevant, prerequisite-missing, metadata-wrong, other); and a redacted defect reference if needed. Do not store raw titles, usernames, ratings, private lists, screenshots, or provider responses in this record. M10.3 owns collection and aggregate reporting once sessions are authorized.
